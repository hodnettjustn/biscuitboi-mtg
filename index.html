MTG Life Counter Update

Update your counter in about 2 minutes

  1. Use a laptop or desktop computer with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. (Phones, Safari and Firefox won't work.)
  2. Plug your counter into the computer with a USB-C data cable. Some charge-only cables won't work. If it isn't detected, try another cable.
  3. Click Install Update below, pick the counter's port in the pop-up, then click Connect and Install.
  4. Wait until it says it's finished. Don't unplug it. The counter restarts by itself.
This browser can't update the counter. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Updates need a secure (https) page.

After the update

  • This update resets your saved settings. After it restarts, open Settings on the counter and tap CALIBRATE TOUCH (about 10 seconds), then set your brightness and screen-off timer again. If you can't reach Settings, hold a finger on the screen while the counter powers on to start calibration.
  • This update lets your counter update itself over Wi-Fi from now on: Settings → UPDATE FIRMWARE.

Trouble?

  • Port doesn't show up: try a different USB cable or port.
  • Stuck on "Connecting": hold the BOOT button on the back of the counter, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again.
  • Still stuck? Message @BiscuitBoiMTG on Instagram.
